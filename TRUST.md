# Trust

Why Zambo is safe to connect to. Every claim here is verifiable.

## Verifiable facts

- **MIT licensed.** The full server source is public at
  <https://github.com/zambodotdev/zambo-mcp>.
- **Zero-auth free tier.** 20 calls per tool per day, no account, no API key.
  There is no credential to leak because none is issued.
- **Official MCP registry.** Listed as `io.github.zambodotdev/zambo`
  (status: active) at <https://registry.modelcontextprotocol.io>.
- **Public reliability scoreboard.** Verified tool runs are published at
  <https://zambo.dev/reliability>. If a tool degrades, it shows up there.
- **Receipts.** Every execution returns a receipt proving the job ran.
  "Type a job, get a receipt" is the contract.
- **One endpoint.** `https://zambo.dev/api/mcp` over HTTPS. No local
  process, no stdio, no shell access to your machine.
- **Solo-built and operated** by Brennan Zambo. No VC, no data broker in
  the loop.

## What Zambo cannot do

- It cannot read your files, your shell, or your environment unless you
  paste them into a tool call yourself.
- It cannot bill you without the explicit Day Pass / subscription flow.
  The free tier never asks for payment details.
