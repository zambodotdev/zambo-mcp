# Move your chat to another AI without starting over

You are talking to one AI and you want to switch to a different one. Normally you would have to explain everything again from zero. With Zambo added, you do not.

## Do this

1. In the AI you are leaving, say: "Use Zambo to wrap up my session."
2. Zambo saves what you were working on: the topic, the decisions, where you left off.
3. Open the other AI (ChatGPT, Grok, Claude, any of them) and say: "Use Zambo to pick up my session."
4. It reads the saved wrap-up and continues like it was there the whole time.

That is the whole trick. Your work follows you from AI to AI instead of living trapped inside one app.

## Why people use this most

- You like one AI for writing and another for research. Move between them freely.
- An app goes down or you switch phones. Your session is saved in Zambo, not in the app.
- You want one memory that every AI you use can share, instead of five AIs that all forget you.
