# Someone sent you a receipt link? Check it in 10 seconds

A Zambo receipt link is proof of AI work that anyone can check. You do not need an account, an app, or any technical skill.

## One receipt

1. Open the link. It starts with zambo.dev/run/
2. Read the verdict at the top. "Verified" means the saved work record matches exactly and was not changed after the fact.
3. Scroll a little to see what ran, when it ran, and a preview of the result.

That is it. Green means the record checks out.

## A whole page full of receipts

Sometimes a page, a report, or a chat contains many receipt links and you want to check all of them at once.

1. Open Receipt Scan: https://zambodotdev.github.io/receipt-scan/
2. Paste the text, drop the page link, or drop a file. Nothing is uploaded; the checking happens in your browser.
3. You get one clear line per receipt: verified, or not found.
4. A sample is loaded on the page so you can try it before trusting it with real links.

## Pass it on

Checking receipts is how you stop taking an AI's word for things. Send the sender back their own verified link when it checks out, and ask questions when it does not.
