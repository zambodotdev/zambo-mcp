# Add Zambo to every AI you use

Zambo works inside the AI apps you already use. You add it once per app, with one URL and no key, and from then on that AI has tools, a memory it can share with your other AIs, and receipts for its work.

## The one link you need

https://zambo.dev/add/?ref=tutorials

Open that page and follow the steps for your app. It takes about a minute per app.

## The simple way to think about it

- Add it to Grok if you talk to Grok.
- Add it to ChatGPT if you work in ChatGPT.
- Add it to Claude if you build with Claude.
- Add it to all of them if you switch around. That is when it gets powerful: what one AI learns, the next one can pick up.

## Your first three tries

1. Ask your AI to do a small real job, then open the receipt link it gives you and check it.
2. Say "Use Zambo to wrap up my session," switch to a different AI, and say "Use Zambo to pick up my session." Watch it continue.
3. Send a receipt link to a friend and let them check your AI's work themselves.
