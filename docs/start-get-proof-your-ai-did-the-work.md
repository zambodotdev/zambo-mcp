# Get proof your AI actually did the work

Your AI says "done." Done how? Did it really do the thing, or does it just sound sure of itself? With Zambo, every job comes with a receipt you can open and check yourself.

## Do this

1. Give your AI a job like you normally would.
2. When it finishes, look for the receipt link. It looks like this: zambo.dev/run/ followed by a code.
3. Open the link. You see what tool ran, when, and what it saved.
4. The page checks the record for you and says "verified" when the saved result matches the record exactly.

## What "verified" means, in plain words

It means the work record was not changed after the fact. What you are looking at is the same result the AI produced at the time, byte for byte. It does not mean the answer was right. It means nobody quietly edited the evidence, including the AI itself.

## When this matters

- A freelancer's AI says the work is delivered. Ask for the receipt.
- Your own AI did something important at 2am. Check the receipt in the morning instead of trusting the summary.
- Anything involving money, files, or messages sent in your name.
